Match & Sort brings 24 games and 15 variants together in one app, with our own original design. Match and pop, tiles and mahjong, sorting, attention and memory games, plus arcade matching; every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements.

• Match and pop: Match Three, Gem Swap Rush, Block Pop, Group Blast, Hex Match, Color Flood, Triple Merge
• Tiles and mahjong: Mahjong Solitaire, Tile Trio, Pile Match, Link Pairs, Domino Twelve, Big Tile Pairs
• Sorting games: Card Sort, Shelf Sort, Yarn Sort, Bottle Crates
• Attention and memory: Picture Pairs, Spot the Difference, Hidden Objects, Common Symbol
• Quick matching: Bubble Popper, Ball Chain, Fruit Merge
• Variant packs: Mahjong Layouts (10), Bubble Variants (5)

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; board size and difficulty grow step by step, and every level is checked to be solvable.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• Hints and undo whenever you need them.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
